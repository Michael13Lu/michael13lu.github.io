Privacy Policy: Legion rclone

Last updated: 2026-11-08

  • Legion rclone is used only by its owner, for personal backups.
  • It requests only the drive.file scope: access to files it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other files.
  • Backup files are encrypted on the owner's server before upload.
  • No data is collected, shared with, or sold to third parties. Nothing is sent anywhere except the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
